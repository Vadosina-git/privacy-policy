Frequently Asked Questions

What is this app?

Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle is a relaxing color puzzle. Starting from the top-left corner, you flood the board with colors, one tap at a time. Fill the whole board with a single color before you run out of moves to win.

How do I play?

Tap a color button to switch your flooded area to that color. Every neighboring cell of the same color joins your area. Plan ahead — you only have a limited number of moves to cover the entire board.

I ran out of moves. What can I do?

You can spend extra moves from your balance to keep playing. Extra moves can be earned by watching an optional rewarded ad or bought as packs in the in-game shop.

Does the app show ads or need an account?

The game shows ads to stay free — no sign-up or account is needed. You can remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase. The puzzle itself works offline; a connection is only needed for ads and purchases.

How do I restore my purchases?

If you bought "Remove Ads" or move packs and reinstalled the app, use the "Restore Purchases" button in the in-game shop. Purchases are tied to your App Store / Google Play account.

Lost my progress?

Progress is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app resets your progress, so we recommend keeping the app installed to preserve your completed levels and statistics.

The game isn't working

Please make sure you're running a supported OS version (iOS 15 or later / Android 8 or later). If the problem persists, contact alzeydi@gmail.com with your device model and OS version.
