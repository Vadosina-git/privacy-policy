Privacy Policy — Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle

Last updated: July 2026

Short version: Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle does not collect your name, email, or any personal profile. It does not require an account. To keep the game free, it shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which — with your consent where required — uses your device's advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. Information We Collect

Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide a name, email address, or any other personal detail. We do not operate our own analytics service and there is no account system. The only data that leaves your device is collected by Google AdMob in order to serve and measure advertising, as described in section 3. The data involved in this app falls into two groups:

  • Local game progress — completed levels, game statistics (wins, losses, best results), extra-move balance, color scheme and other settings. Stored on your device only; never transmitted to us.
  • Advertising data (via Google AdMob) — to display and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect your advertising identifier and related device and interaction data, as described in section 3. This processing is governed by your consent (where required) and device settings.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (campaign levels, statistics, extra moves, and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle displays advertisements through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that grant extra moves. To serve, personalize, and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process the following data:

  • Advertising identifiers — IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID (GAID) on Android.
  • Coarse (approximate, IP-based) location — country / city level, used to serve and measure regionally relevant ads.
  • App and ad interaction data — ad impressions, clicks, and basic in-app events used for frequency capping, measurement, and third-party advertising. Google may also use this data for its own analytics of ad performance.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics — technical reports used only to keep the app working. This data is not used to track you and is not linked to your identity.

We do not sell this data. For more on how Google uses advertising data, see the Google Advertising Policy.

3.1 GDPR Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform, "UMP") before any ad is requested, asking whether you allow personalized advertising, only non-personalized advertising, or no data sharing with ad partners beyond what is strictly necessary. No ad loads until this form has been answered.

You can reopen this consent form at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy inside the app. Uninstalling the app clears your locally stored consent state. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal.

3.2 Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, before the app or its advertising partners can use your IDFA for tracking, iOS shows a system App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog asking whether you allow tracking. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Flood It. If you deny tracking, ads are still shown but are non-personalized.

3.3 Opting Out of Personalized Ads at the Device Level

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for Flood It.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" and use "Reset advertising ID".

4. In-App Purchases

The app offers optional in-app purchases (such as packs of extra moves and an option to remove advertising). All purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not receive, store, or process any payment information — no card numbers, billing addresses, or store account details ever reach us. Purchase entitlements (e.g. "ads removed") are stored locally on your device and can be restored through the store's standard restore-purchases mechanism.

5. Internet Connection

Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle can be played offline. An internet connection is required only to load advertisements, the consent form described above, and to process in-app purchases.

6. Children's Privacy

Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). Our ad requests are configured so that AdMob does not treat traffic from this app as directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the advertising features, contact us at the email address below and we will take appropriate steps.

7. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — for collecting and managing advertising consent (GDPR / regional privacy requirements).
  • Apple App Store / Google Play billing — for processing optional in-app purchases.

The app does not integrate Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any other behavioral-tracking or attribution SDK beyond the AdMob advertising components described above. There is no real-money gambling and no user-generated content in the app.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK/Switzerland), CCPA (California), and similar regulations, including the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, object to its processing, withdraw consent to personalized advertising at any time (see section 3.1–3.3), and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Because Flood It: Colors Relax Puzzle stores all gameplay data locally on your device and collects no personal data on its own servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly: deleting the app removes all local data. For advertising data held by Google, you may also exercise rights directly with Google.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
