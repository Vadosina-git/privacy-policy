Privacy Policy

Bubble Words Merge Puzzle (com.khrlaz.bubbles)  ·  Last updated: August 8, 2026

Short version: Bubble Words Merge Puzzle ("Bubble Words", "we", "our", "the app") does not collect your name, email, or any personal profile. It does not require an account. To keep the game free, it shows advertisements through Google AdMob, which — with your consent where required — uses your device's advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads. The game requires an internet connection to load ads.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Bubble Words Merge Puzzle ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application available on iOS and Android. The App is developed and published by Khralz ("the Developer").

This policy applies to the App on all platforms where it is distributed. By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the App.

2. Information We Collect

Bubble Words does not ask you to sign up, log in, or provide a name, email address, or any other personal detail. We do not operate our own analytics service and there is no account system. The only data that leaves your device is collected by Google AdMob in order to serve and measure advertising, as described in section 4. The data involved in this app falls into two groups:

  • Local game progress — completed levels, hint/energy balance, and settings. Stored on your device only; never transmitted to us.
  • Advertising data (via Google AdMob) — to display and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect your advertising identifier and related device and interaction data, as described in section 4. This processing is governed by your consent (where required) and device settings.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your game progress (completed levels, hints/energy, and settings) is saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, Bubble Words displays advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve, personalize, and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process the following data:

  • Advertising identifiers — IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID (GAID) on Android.
  • Coarse (approximate, IP-based) location — country / city level, used to serve and measure regionally relevant ads.
  • App and ad interaction data — ad impressions, clicks, and basic in-app events used for frequency capping, measurement, and third-party advertising. Google may also use this data for its own analytics of ad performance.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics — technical reports used only to keep the app working. This data is not used to track you and is not linked to your identity.

We do not sell this data. For more on how Google uses advertising data, see the Google Advertising Policy.

4.1 GDPR Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app shows a consent form (powered by Google's User Messaging Platform, "UMP") before any ad is requested, asking whether you allow personalized advertising, only non-personalized advertising, or no data sharing with ad partners beyond what is strictly necessary. No ad loads until this form has been answered.

You can reopen this consent form at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy inside the app. Uninstalling the app clears your locally stored consent state. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before withdrawal.

4.2 Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, before the app or its advertising partners can use your IDFA for tracking, iOS shows a system App Tracking Transparency (ATT) dialog asking whether you allow tracking. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Bubble Words. If you deny tracking, ads are still shown but are non-personalized.

4.3 Opting Out of Personalized Ads at the Device Level

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → turn off "Personalized Ads"; and Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable for Bubble Words.
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization" and use "Reset advertising ID".

5. Internet Connection

Bubble Words requires an internet connection to load advertisements and the consent form described above. Without a network connection, ads may fail to load. Gameplay itself does not require a connection and no game progress is uploaded when you are online.

6. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Internet / network access — required to load advertisements and the consent dialog.
  • Advertising identifier (AD_ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) — used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent and device settings as described in section 4.
  • Vibration (Android) — for haptic feedback when bubbles merge or a level is completed. Can be disabled in the device settings.

The App does not request access to your microphone, camera, precise location, contacts, photos, files, or calendar.

7. In-App Purchases

The app does not currently offer in-app purchases. If in-app purchases are added in the future, they will be processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store; we would not receive, store, or process any payment information. This policy will be updated before any such feature is enabled.

8. Children's Privacy

Bubble Words is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in the relevant jurisdiction). Our ad requests are configured so that AdMob does not treat traffic from this app as directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the advertising features, contact us at the email address below and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. See Google Privacy Policy and Google Advertising Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — for collecting and managing advertising consent (GDPR / regional privacy requirements).

The app does not integrate Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any other behavioral-tracking or attribution SDK beyond the AdMob advertising components described above. There is no real-money gambling and no user-generated content in the app.

10. Data Retention

Local game data persists on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data in the device settings. Advertising identifiers and ad-related event data are retained by Google and its partners according to their own retention policies (typically up to 14 months for measurement data — see the Google Advertising Policy). We do not retain advertising data on our own servers, because we do not operate any servers for this App.

11. Data Security

Local game data is protected by your device's operating-system sandbox. Advertising data is transmitted to Google over encrypted connections and handled under Google's security practices. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, the data we hold directly cannot be breached on our side.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under privacy laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK/Switzerland), CCPA (California), and similar regulations, including the right to access, rectify, or erase your data, object to its processing, withdraw consent to personalized advertising at any time (see sections 4.1–4.3), and lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. Because Bubble Words stores all gameplay data locally on your device and collects no personal data on its own servers, you can exercise most of these rights directly: deleting the app removes all local data. For advertising data held by Google, you may also exercise rights directly with Google.

13. International Transfers

Game data is not transmitted off your device. Advertising data processed by Google AdMob may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own, in accordance with Google's data-transfer safeguards described in the Google Privacy Policy.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
